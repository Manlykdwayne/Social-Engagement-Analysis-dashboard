# Social-Engagement-Analysis-dashboard
Social Engagement Analysis Dashboard, built by Philip Simeon-Popoola in Power BI, gives a single-view snapshot of how audiences engage with content across three social platforms: Instagram, Facebook, and Twitter

# Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) Report
Purpose: This report translates the dashboard's patterns into plain-language findings for stakeholders and non-technical teams, no data background required.

## Overall Engagement Snapshot
Out of 300K total interactions, likes dominate at 238K (about 79% of all engagement), followed by shares (42K) and comments (20K, the smallest share). This tells us the audience is quick to show approval (a like) far more often than they're willing to actively comment or share — a common pattern, but worth noting when setting realistic goals for comment-driven campaigns.

## Timing: When Does Engagement Happen?
The hourly trend line shows likes spike sharply at several points throughout the day, with peaks scattered between roughly 6 AM and 9 PM, rather than one single best time. Shares and comments stay consistently low and flat all day, meaning timing mainly affects likes, not the more active engagement types. For scheduling purposes, this suggests posting throughout waking hours is reasonable, but comment/share activity won't be strongly boosted by time of day alone.

## Content Format: What Works Best?
Video content drives by far the most engagement, especially likes (peaking near 60K), before dropping off sharply for carousel and poll posts, and leveling out at a lower, steady rate for text and image posts (around 20K).

Takeaway for content teams: video is the strongest-performing format and should be prioritized where resources allow; text and image posts still generate steady, if lower, engagement.

## Sentiment: How Do Audiences Feel?
Engagement skews positive overall, with positive reactions outweighing negative and neutral ones across all three platforms. Facebook and Instagram show a larger share of positive sentiment, while negative sentiment is present but consistently smaller. Neutral sentiment is the lowest across the board.
Takeaway: the brand's current content is landing well with audiences, and there's no red flag of a sentiment crisis on any single platform.

## Platform Comparison
Instagram is the top-performing platform, generating the highest total engagement, followed by Facebook, with Twitter trailing noticeably behind both. This pattern holds consistently across both platform-focused charts, reinforcing that Instagram and Facebook should likely remain the primary focus for engagement-driven strategy, while Twitter may need a different approach or reduced investment.

## Day of Week: When Is the Audience Most Active?
Engagement is highest midweek, Wednesday leads, followed closely by Friday and Sunday, then Thursday and Tuesday at similar moderate levels. Saturday and Monday are the weakest days by a wide margin, roughly half the engagement of the top days.
Takeaway: scheduling key content for Wednesday, Friday, or Sunday is likely to maximize reach; Monday and Saturday posts may need extra promotion or lower priority.

# Summary of Key Recommendations

Positive sentiment dominates	Continue current content tone/strategy

Monday & Saturday underperform	Deprioritize or test new tactics on these days
